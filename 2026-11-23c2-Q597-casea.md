## Theorem (case a closes)
Let (x,y) be a diverging B/C pair, and let L be its class-1 a-step role, with L's re-run taking (1,a_T'). Then (Ea)
holds (neither L nor its re-run stops after one middle step), and so does (Stale_a) (no hexagon of A after p+2 meets
X_{p+2} \ Y_{p+2}). By Prop casea (11-22 c2) this gives BS(L) and (Ca) in case a. So **(Role) <= BS(L) on the re-run-b
(geometric) records only**.

## Proof in four pieces
1. **Lemma grid** (by hand, from `migrate.py`). A journey toward T stops only where f0_T(min R) = f0_T(X_T) and
   f1_T(min R) = f1_T(X_T). Here f0_T is the Λ0 form with kernel u_T, and f1_T is the Λ1 form with kernel w_T:
   f0_B = a0+b0, f1_B = a1+b1, f0_C = b0, f1_C = b1.
2. **Lemma gridmono** (finite, `grid.py`). F_ε = f_ε(min R) is monotone along T-journeys: non-decreasing for B,
   non-increasing for C. (1,a0) moves F0 strictly and fixes F1; (1,a1) does the reverse.
   **Theorem Ea:** A' leaves R_p by a_T', which moves the functional that A's a_T fixes. So R_p, and with it R_{p+1},
   is off the grid in that coordinate. The symmetric argument covers A' at R'_{p+1}. It uses only the two step-p types.
3. **Lemma dcomp** (one finite check, `dcomp.py`). D = W ∪ (non-rh tiles made by A at p, p+1). The re-run's
   hexagons meet, outside A's, only J_o-created tiles (6/6 in all 8 placements). Lesion: with J_o's removed tiles
   instead, it fails 8/8.
4. **Prop wake (W_a)** (by hand + 4 placement constants). A reads no wake tile at k ≥ p+2.
   - j ≥ c0 (T=B) or j ≥ c0+2 (T=C): Lemma phi's g separates the two journeys.
   - j < c0: λ is monotone on both journeys, removed tiles have λ-offset in [0, max Q], and one coordinate of
     λ(R_{p+2}) − λ(R^J_{c0}) is 1 where max Q_T' is 0.
   - T=C, j ∈ {c0, c0+1}: one leftover triangle, with λ1 below λ1(R_{p+2}).
   Then no-revisit disposes of the A-made tiles, and the result is (Stale_a).
