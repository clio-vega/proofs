## Theorem statements
- **Standing hypotheses (S).** Termination and the engine rule, plus **(Mos)**: M0, M0·J_x and M0·J_y are mosaics M0(ℓ) of KT puzzles with 0<m0<n.
  - (Mos) is now stated explicitly and is not proved.
  - Its evidence is Purbhoo 0705.1184 §2.4 and the census bridge (520/520 at size 5, 719/719 on the size-6 subsample).
- **Lemma both (new, proved).**
  - (1) A diverging role's journey and its re-run first differ at a step where both take a step. By Lemma class every rhombus is of the nest-A class or of T's class 1, and Lemma term makes the stop test agree there.
  - (2) If one role diverges, so does the other. Use Thm fc, Lemma nr and Lemma dinv.
- **Prop dich (proved).** In every diverging B/C pair exactly one role L is at (1,a_T), and L's re-run takes (1,b) (case b) or (1,a_T') (case a). No other case exists.
- **Thm bsall (proved under (S)).** BS(L) and (Ca) hold for **every** diverging B/C pair. Case b is Thm bs (11-25), case a is Thm casea (11-23 c2).
- **Hidden hypothesis found.** Thm main (11-22 c2) uses Lemma mixed, which assumes the composite boards are equal, i.e. (MC) for the pair. So **(Role) is proved only under (MC)**.
- **Thm roleL (proved, unconditional).** Here Δ_L = δ(L's re-run) − δ(L). Δ_L = 0 in case a. Δ_L ≠ 0 in case b, supported on side s.
- **Prop mcE (proved, given PQ-RIGID).** The composite boards are equal ⟺ Δ_x = Δ_y.
- **First unproved link: (G3′), Δ_E = Δ_L** for the other role E. It is equivalent to (MC) on B/C pairs. The data: 779/779 at size 5, 1232/1232 on fresh size-6 chunks, B/B 0/440 and 0/661.

## Step 0 (monodromy, computed)
- The loop: a single-rhombus commutor loop. Move x, then y, then undo x's nest-B cell first, then y's.
- It closes on every B/C pair (1558) and every commuting B/B pair (339).
- On the non-commuting B/B pairs it **never** closes: 336 open, 104 undefined.
- The two orders occupy different nest-B cell sets in 440/440 pairs. In the open loops the first undo lands at corner y (336/336).
- So the loop measures commutation of cells. It is **not** Purbhoo's flock-level monodromy, where the standard order fixes the rhombus order. This is not a counterexample to the jdt prediction.
