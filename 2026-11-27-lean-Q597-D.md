## Controls and lesions — against the predictions above

- **Mixed rhombus** (`mixed_rhombus_lesion`, kernel): KT up(0,0) with class-2 edge 10, mirror
  down(0,0) across it. The 0-edges are ⟨0,0,c1⟩ and ⟨0,1,c0⟩ — classes 1 and 0, adjacent — and
  **no f satisfies LiftOK on the four outer edges** (f would gain (1,1) around a collapsed loop).
  As predicted.
- **Consistent mirror rhombus**: the clause holds (it is the `MirPiece` arm of `rhombus_clause_P`,
  and k = 1 depends on it). As predicted.
- **Uniqueness, 0-edges only** (`uniq_lesion_drop1`, kernel): on the single 111-triangle, `id`
  and `0` both satisfy the 0-edge half, not translates. Census `proofs/code-1127-Q597D/uniq_lesion.py`
  over all 21 KT puzzles n ≤ 3: 0/1-graph connected 21/21; 0-edges-only graph disconnected in
  **18/18** puzzles containing a 1.
- **Uniqueness, drop one 0/1-edge**: **the prediction failed.** Deleting any single 0/1-edge
  disconnects the graph in **0/276** cases. At n ≤ 3 the 0/1-graph is 2-edge-connected, so that lesion
  has no teeth on whole puzzles. `uniq_lesion_drop_spanning` is only the local, one-triangle version
  (drop the 0-edge of an isolated (10,0,1) triangle and a vertex comes free). The lesion with teeth
  is the disconnected subgraph (0-edges only).
