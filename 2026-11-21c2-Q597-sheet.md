# Q597 G1a — the sheet statement (PROVE 2026-11-21 c2)

Full write-up: `proofs/2026-11-21c2-Q597-sheet.tex` (pdf beside it). Code: `proofs/code-1121c2-Q597/`.

## Theorem (proved, given one computed contact fact)
**(Sheet) ⇐ (FC≤) ∧ (Sh1).**
- **(Sheet).** On a clean nontrivial B/C record, the clean journey A never reads (removes) a wake tile of J_o (W = M0 \ M0·J_o) that J_o removed by a **class-1 a-step**, i.e. by a step that neither starts nor ends on sheet 0.
- **(Sh1), the handshake.** A's steps p and p+1 read only J_o's steps c0 and c0+1. Then R_{p+2} = R^o_{c0+2}, of class 0, and step c0 is not a class-1 a-step.

## Proof outline
1. **Lemma shared** (finite check). The class-0 sheet is shared by B and C: they have the same good hexagon of each type, and the same images for a0 and a1. For b the images differ (240° vs 120°).
2. **Lemma lock** (by hand). Two terminating journeys on the same class-0 rhombus, where the other journey's hexagon is present on this board, take the same step.
3. **Lemma bc** (by hand). Along the shadow the hexagon hypothesis holds automatically. This uses no-revisit, Lemma nr, Prop fc and (Sh1).
4. **Lemma fin** (frames). No journey ends on sheet 0, so J_o leaves sheet 0 by a class-0 b-step e. At e the clean journey takes the same hexagon with the other rotation: this is the **split**.
5. **Prop sh3.** After the split the clean journey removes no tile that J_o removes.
   - **Future, by hand.** Put g(R) = a0+b0 of the lex-min vertex. It is non-decreasing toward B and non-increasing toward C. It is +1 at B's class-0 b-step and 0 at C's. Removed-tile offsets are {0} for B and {−1,0} for C. So the two post-split wakes lie on opposite sides of a level line.
   - **Past, computer-assisted.** 4-offset monotonicity gives a window (≤5 steps for A, ≤7 for J). Over all words in that window, the two unions of removed tiles are disjoint.
