## Theorems (all proved, unconditional, for M0 = Z^4 lift of a KT puzzle + bubble nest rhombi, 0 < m0 < n)
- **Lemma chir.** In every unit triangle, the ccw class order is (0,2,1). In a KT rhombus with 0-edges of class i, the 1-edges have class i−1 and the diagonal class i+1.
- **Lemma D** (KT, statement in Purbhoo 0705.1184 §2.4). The edge vectors (d,0) / (0,d) / (−d0,−d1) form a well-defined closed cochain, so the lift L exists and is unique up to translation. m_k is the same on all 3 sides. f_k = π_k∘L is a translation on k-triangles. The boundary goes monotonically onto ∂Δ_k, with Δ_k = m_k·T_1.
- **Thm G1** (signed edge count). Let N_k(t) be the number of Λ_k-lifts of t. Then N_k(t) = 1 on Δ_k and 0 off it. The proof needs no PL topology and no chirality.
- **Cor img.** Every vertex of every M0 tile has π_k ∈ Δ_k.
- **Thm G2** (column graph). No rhombus lies over an interior E (Lemma N: nest rhombi lie in the side rectangle and so over ∂Δ_k). The squares over E form one ν-monotone column from τ⁻ to τ⁺, and chirality fixes the step at n_i (k=0: (−1,1), (−1,0), (0,−1); k=1: (0,1), (−1,1), (−1,0)).
- **Thm FB** (half-plane bound). For every nest frame and all i, j ∈ Z, the cells (−1,j) and (i,−1) miss every tile. Along each run, a linear functional is ≤ 0 on the vertices of every M0 tile and ≥ 0 on the cell. φ(M0) ⊆ z0Δ0 + z1Δ1, which is Purbhoo's hexagon.
- **Cor.** With Corollary glob of the c1 tex: (A3), (E1)=(Tn), WS-b and (Comp)(i) hold for geometric records. The earlier chain was not re-verified this session.
