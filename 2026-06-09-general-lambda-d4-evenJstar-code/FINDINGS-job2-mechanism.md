## The mechanism: J\* is an affine 2-adic box

For **every** tie shape (λ ≠ unique-min), the minimiser set is

> **`J* = j₀ + { Σ_{a∈S} 2ᵃ : A ⊆ S }`** — an affine "box" generated by **distinct
> powers of two**, exponents `a ≥ 1`.

Verified **4318/4318 ties to m ≤ 14** (`is_pow2box`). Hence `|J*| = 2^{|S|}` — always
a power of two, which is why we only ever see sizes 1, 2, 4. The observed generator
sets are `S ⊆ {1,2,3}` (powers 2, 4, 8); distribution over m≤12 ties:

```
S={1}:978  S={2}:376  S={1,2}:245  S={3}:12  S={1,3}:12  S={2,3}:1
```

## The involution (fixed-point-free ⟹ even count)

Pick the smallest generator `g = 2^{min S}`. The map

> **σ(j) = j + g  if bit (j−j₀) at min S is 0, else j − g**  (toggle membership of
> the smallest generator in the subset A)

is a **fixed-point-free involution of J\*** that preserves `val`. **1624/1624 ties
(m≤12).** A fixed-point-free involution forces `|J*|` even. ∎ (mechanism)

This also re-derives, cleanly, the F₂ fact: each `j∈J*` contributes a unit
`u_j(−i)^{v_j}iʲ`, and in `ℤ[i]/(1+i) ≅ 𝔽₂` every unit ≡ 1, so the leading
π-coefficient `≡ |J*| (mod π)`. Even `|J*|` ⟹ leading-order cancellation.

## What drives the box — `v₂(M_j)`, not the binomial

- The binomial part alone (`w(j)=j+2v₂(C(m,j))`) **never** reproduces J\*: 0/1624.
- The box lives in `v₂(M_j)`. Example `λ=(6,3,1,1,1)`, m=6: `v₂(M_j)` on even j is
  `4,3,2,1` — **drops exactly 1 per step of 2 in j** — so `j+2v₂(M_j)` is constant
  ⟹ full box `{0,2,4,6}`. Where the drop is irregular, the box is a sparser
  subcube, e.g. `(8,2,2)` gives `{0,4}` (generator 4).

## The crisp lever for PROVE — exact valuation step law

On every involution pair `{j, j+2ᵃ}` (a ∈ S) of every tie:

> **`v₂(C(m,j) M_j) − v₂(C(m,j+2ᵃ) M_{j+2ᵃ}) = 2^{a-1}`** — exact.

Verified **2398/2398 pairs (m≤12)**; on the 1010 pairs where `v₂(C(m,·))` is constant
across the pair, the law sharpens to `v₂(M_j) − v₂(M_{j+2ᵃ}) = 2^{a-1}` (1010/1010).

**This is the structural lever:** proving the 2-adic step law (a Kummer/MN statement
about `C(m,j)M_j`) proves the box, hence the involution, hence even-|J\*|, hence
leading-order cancellation for all non-(2,2) shapes. The data favours attacking
`v₂(M_j)` directly via a Kummer/Lucas formula for `M_j = ⟨s_λ,h₁^{2(m−j)}e₂ʲ⟩`.
