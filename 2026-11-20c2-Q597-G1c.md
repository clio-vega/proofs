# Q597 G1c — the ascending neighbour of a swept tile is never swept (PROVE 2026-11-20 c2)

Tex/PDF: `proofs/2026-11-20c2-Q597-G1c.{tex,pdf}`. Code: `proofs/code-1120c2-Q597/`.

## Statement
**Theorem AW.** Take a terminating journey toward B or C in a Purbhoo mosaic. Let u be a non-rhombus tile removed at step j, and let v be an *ascending* edge vector of R_j, meaning ψ(u+v) = ψ(u) + e_c. Then u+v is removed at no step k ≠ j, and u+v ∉ H_j.

**Corollary (G1c).** For an (A)-tile u, assume (G1a-A): −α is an ascending edge vector of the rhombus at the step that removes u (computed 1639/1639). Then no step of x's journey J_o removes u−α.

## Proof outline
1. **Step 0 of the brief fails, 0/1639.** ψ(u−α) ≤ μ_0 always. The reason is structural: u−α lies above u in ψ, which is where the rhombus would have swept in the past. ψ only carries the future.
2. **Lemma W (dip), by hand.** A tile of H_k with ψ_c < μ_c has ψ_c = μ_c − 1 = μ_{k+1,c}. Proof: check the three hexagon types; π₀H is s₀ (a1), a unit triangle (b), or a unit parallelogram whose far side is s₀′ (a0).
3. **Theorem F, by hand.** Removal is ψ-antitone: if u is removed at j and t at k > j, then ψ(t) ≤ μ_k ≤ μ_{j+1} ≤ ψ(u). This gives the future half for every ascending translate.
4. **Lemma word.** Each (allowed class, type a0/a1/b) has exactly one good configuration. So a journey is a word in {a0, a1, b}, and the word determines everything.
5. **Lemma window.** The 4-offset f(i) = (ψ(u) − μ_i, A(u) − λ_i) is monotone in each coordinate. Each non-stationary step moves it by at least 1. Only one kind of b-step is stationary, and two stationary steps are never consecutive. The removal offsets lie in Q_T, with L¹-spread at most 5. Hence |j − k| ≤ 11.
6. **Exhaustive enumeration.** `words2.py 12` checks all words of length ≤ 12, from both classes and for both T: 3,188,808 nodes, 33,480,864 step pairs, **0 violations**. The control (ascending, but not an edge vector of R_j) fires 708,600 times.
7. **Props strip and strip′: hand proofs for all triangles.** Strip covers (B, Λ₀), and by Σ (C, Λ₁). The swept cells form a path whose index rises by exactly 1 per cell and whose strip is monotone. Translating by v moves one strip against the path's direction, so the translate is never on the path. Strip′ is the mirrored case (B, Λ₁), and by Σ (C, Λ₀), with κ = ι + 2·strip. Only squares rest on the enumeration alone.
