With `α = w (s-1-B)`, `β = w (s-B)`, `γ = w (s-A)`, `δ = w (s-A+1)` — so `β, γ` are the two
ends of the window at `s`, and `α, δ` the two sites just outside it:

```
W s ^ 2 - W (s-1) * W (s+1) = W s * (β + γ - α - δ) + (α - γ) * (β - δ)
```

**This identity was a prose comment in `Convolution.lean` until now.** The whole of route (b)
rests on it, and it was carrying an unverified "the algebra of the obstruction is" in a file
whose other claims are all theorems. It is a theorem now.

## What is refuted: route (b)'s two inequalities are insufficient, not surplus

The registry node recorded the live worry that route (b)'s two extra inequalities *"may be true,
provable and entirely surplus."* That was the wrong worry. They are **insufficient**.

The two inequalities are `β·γ ≥ α·δ` (monotone-ratio form of PF₂ read at the window ends) and
`W s ≥ β + γ` (the window carries both of its own ends). Add nonnegativity of all four end
values, of `W s`, **and** of both neighbours `W (s±1)`. The conclusion still does not follow:

> **Witness** `(α, β, γ, δ, S) = (10, 1, 1, 0, 2)`.
> `β·γ = 1 ≥ 0 = α·δ` ✓ · `β + γ = 2 ≤ 2 = S` ✓ · `W (s-1) = 11 ≥ 0` ✓ · `W (s+1) = 1 ≥ 0` ✓
> and yet `S² = 4 < 11 = W (s-1)·W (s+1)`. Identity RHS: `2·(-8) + 9·1 = -7`.

So formalising those two inequalities on their own — the work the brief flagged as possibly
wasted — could **never** have closed the goal. This is a sharper verdict than "surplus", and
it is the opposite kind of error: not a true step that isn't load-bearing, but a hypothesis set
that cannot bear the load at all.

## `prop:regI` is not refuted — and the reason says what a real proof needs

The witness is **not realisable** by a genuine PF₂ sequence. Over ℤ, `β/α = 1/10` forces every
later ratio to be `≤ 1/10`, so `w (s-B+1) = 0`; interval support then kills everything to the
right, so `γ = 0` unless the window has width 1 — and at width 1 the ends coincide, `β = γ = 1`
with `S = 1`, violating `β + γ ≤ S`. Either way the witness dies.

**But look at what kills it:** an argument about the window's *interior* and the *global*
monotonicity of the ratio sequence. Route (b)'s two inequalities see only the two ends and the
total. That is exactly the information they discard, which is why they cannot work. This is the
2026-10-02 lesson applied in the right direction: *test the intermediate in the class your
hypotheses carve out*, not on instances. On instances route (b)'s hypotheses are green
56022/56022. In the abstract class they carve out, they are false.
