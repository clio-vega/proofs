## What the three instances share (proto-statement)

Let `F_c(a, b) ∈ ℤ[a, b]` be a family of integer-coefficient polynomials in `(a, b)`,
parameterized by an integer `c`. Assume:

- (A) **Parity-sheet content**. `content_ε(F_c) := min_{ (a,b) ∈ ℤ² : a ≡ b ≡ ε mod 2 } v_2( F_c(a,b) )`
  is well-defined and finite (i.e. F_c is not identically zero on the sheet, and takes a
  minimum since v_2 is bounded above on nonzero polynomials modulo a shift).

- (B) **Resonance factor**. There exists an integer `K(c)` — a polynomial in `c` — such that
  `F_c(a,b) = K(c) · F̃_c(a,b) + (coefficient-lower-order terms)`, where the leading `K(c)` piece
  captures the "highest-degree-in-c" coefficient of the polynomial after change of variables
  `a = 2P + b + δ` (parity split).

- (C) **Ceiling from residual polynomial**. Let `M(c) := v_2( gcd of coefficients of F_c after
  parity-split change of variables )`. `M(c)` is bounded above by a constant `C_F` depending
  only on the polynomial family (not on `c`), because the coefficients themselves are polynomials
  in `c` of bounded degree.

Then

> **(SHARED CONTENT LEMMA — conjectural)**
>
> `content_ε(F_c) = min( v_2(K(c)), C_F ) + shift_ε`
>
> where `shift_ε ∈ ℤ_{≥0}` is a small parity-dependent adjustment (0 or 1) determined by whether
> `ε` hits the resonance sheet or an odd offset.

The lemma splits the content into an **uncapped resonance tower** `v_2(K(c))` (which grows with
`v_2(c)`, `v_2(c−α)`, …) and a **hard ceiling** `C_F` from the polynomial's own coefficients.

   directly computable as `min(v_2(K_β(c)), C_β)` for some `K_β, C_β` — and Rick's formula
   is exactly this shape reduced to digit-sum form.** This is strong external evidence.

## What I need to prove (proof strategy — still vague)

**Step 1 — nail the polynomial normal form.** Given a three-row polynomial family
`F_c(a, b) ∈ ℤ[a, b]` arising from `⟨s_λ, h_1^{2m-2j} e_2^j⟩` calculations, express it in the
form `F_c(a, b) = Σ_{i,j} α_{ij}(c) · (a-b+1)^i · (2P+…)^j` where P = (a-b-something)/2.
This is the "change of variables to parity sheet" that all my c=1..5 boundary proofs used.

**Step 2 — bound the leading-coefficient ceiling.** Each α_{ij}(c) is a polynomial in `c`.
The **combined leading-coefficient tower** `v_2( gcd_{i,j} α_{ij}(c) )` is bounded above by
`max_{i,j} v_2( α_{ij}(c) )` in the leading `(i, j)`. This gives `C_F` — the hard ceiling.

**Step 3 — identify the resonance polynomial `K(c)`.** The 2-adic content of `F_c` on the
parity sheet is bounded below by `v_2( K(c) )` where `K(c)` is the sub-leading coefficient
term. This is a **content-of-polynomial** bound and lifts to a `min` when both bounds are
tight, which they are on the parity sheet by a general principle I still need to write down.

**Step 4 — verify against instances**. Check the three (four with β') instances by direct
computation. Already done for Clio ♣ (c ≤ 26), Clio γ (c ≤ 10), Lyra cap (c ≤ 26), Rick β'
(c ≤ 15 with formula, c ≤ 11 with proof machinery).
