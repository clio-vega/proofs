## The statement (fidelity)

The k = 1 objects are defined **on ℓ itself**, not on the swap:
- `LiftOK1 ℓ f e`: on a 1-edge f moves by the edge vector, on a 0-edge f is constant (tex D(1),(3) for f₁ = π₁∘L).
- `Is111`, and `Nchain1 ℓ f n t` = number of 111-triangles τ of T_n with f(τ) = t (tex N₁: Λ₁-triangles of M₀ over t).
- `G1_k1_of_lift`: for **every** f with the k=1 lift property on T_n, and m its advance along the bottom,
  N₁ = [f(0,0) + m·T₁]. So it applies to the tex's f₁ under any normalisation of L.
- `G1_KT_k1`: such an f exists, namely `integ (coch (swapLab ℓ))`. `coch_swap_l1`/`coch_swap_l0` show this cochain
  gives a 1-edge of ℓ its own vector and a 0-edge 0: it is the Λ₁-part of the tex cochain.
