### S6 verified first-hand, with two caveats he does not state

I fetched the e-print rather than accept his summary, because this is the claim I most wanted
to be true.

- **The quotation is accurate.** `Half-line-TASEP-final.tex` L454, verbatim: *"This initial

  proposed as Conjecture 5.5 in [garbali_symmetric_2025] at the level of the six-vertex model."*
- **The citation resolves to my paper.** Their `.bbl` gives `garbali_symmetric_2025` =
  Garbali, de Gier, Mead, Wheeler, *Symmetric Functions from the Six-Vertex Model in
  Half-Space*, *Ann. Henri Poincaré* 26(7):2557–2624, 2025, arXiv:**2312.14348** — i.e. GDMW.
- **Locators:** statement at the lemma labelled `lm: orthogonality` (L458; Lemma 2.4 by the §2

**Caveat (i), and it is the one that matters.** What they prove is the **homogeneous** version.
My application has functionals acting on the very `y`-parameters an inhomogeneous statement
would carry. So *whether the homogeneous case suffices for my Theorem 2 is exactly the open
question*, not a step that can be assumed. S6 is a **candidate route, not a closure**, and I
have graded it so.

**Caveat (ii).** They prove it for half-space ASEP/TASEP, whereas the sentence locates
Conjecture 5.5 *"at the level of the six-vertex model"*. A transfer is still required.

**An observation of my own, offered as a connection and not as a result.** Their orthogonality
measure (eq `orthog t=0`) carries
`∏_{i<j} (1 − q w_i w_j)/(1 − w_i w_j)` and `∏_i (1 − q w_i²)/(…)`.
Those are exactly the **long-root** factors `w_iw_j − 1` whose order governs my Q488 jump
criterion, and `(1 − q w_i²)` is precisely a denominator factor of my type-C collapse
multiplier `ρ^C = (1−q)²/((1 − qw_a²)(1 − qw_b²))`, i.e. the `h = 2` case. Their boundary rate
`α` appears in `(q + α − 1 − α w_i)`. Both their measure and my multiplier are built from
boundary data on the same divisor family. I do not know yet whether that is a coincidence of
shape; it is the first thing I would compute.
