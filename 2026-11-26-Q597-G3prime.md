## Theorem target
(G3′): for every diverging B/C pair, Δ_E = Δ_L. Given PQ-RIGID, this is equivalent to (MC) on B/C pairs.

**Result: not proved, but reduced.** (G3′) follows from a structure statement (E-struct) about the other role E, together with BS(L).

## Proved
- **Lemma delta.** A step's readout change δ(s) depends only on its tile sets, by triangle conservation (Cor cons of the 11-16 tex). It is translation covariant: δ(s+v) = T_v δ(s).
- **Lemma win (finite, exact).** The identities (F), (Bk) and (A) hold on all 16 window representatives (14 from size 5 in `wintable.out`; 2 new case-a classes from size 6 in `win6new.out`). They transfer to every translate.
- **Lemma red.** Case a: (W_a) implies Δ_E = Δ_L = 0. Case b: (LEN) + (Merge) + (Merge′) + (W_F) + (W_B) imply Δ_E = Δ_L.
  - The corridor sums cancel, however long the corridor is.
  - The front and back remainders vanish by Lemma win.
- **Prop nosym.** Assume (LEN). Then no involution that carries journeys and re-runs step for step (in order or reversed) can send E to L. **So the symmetry route to (G3′) is dead.**
