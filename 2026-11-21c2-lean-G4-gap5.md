## Gap 5: which form, and why
There were two candidate forms. (i) "Same path ⇒ values transport": `X = g ∘ Y` with `g` monotone and `Y` arbitrary.
(ii) The invariant `X = g ∘ std X`, written `DestdBy g S X`.
**I chose (ii).** `lemmaA_slides` is already stated against `std S X`, so (ii) composes with it directly, and the only new fact needed is
that one move preserves the invariant. That fact is `std_move` plus `moveX_eq_src`. Monotonicity of `g` never becomes a
hypothesis. It is built in, because `g` acts on `std`-ranks and those ranks carry `X`'s (value, column) order by construction.
Form (i) would have to prove path agreement again for arbitrary `Y`, and with a weakly monotone `g` the tie-break would need care.
