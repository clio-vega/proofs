## What Φ is in Lean
Φ is built from the same objects that the thm:G4 assembly consumes. It is not a parallel implementation.
- **Item 1.** The ranking is `StdSlide.tilde`, the very function that `lemmaC` speaks about. It is the reverse standard
  order (ties broken by column) after `rot`, and it is 0-based.
- **Item 2.** `g = u_{A^∨}` has support `skewCells (compl R C A) []`, where `compl` gives row `r` the length `C − A_{R−1−r}`.
  Its values are `uVal y = y.1 + 1`.
- **Item 3.** The slides are `StdSlide.slides revRule (R+C)`: the larger of W/S, tie → south. These are the slides of
  `lemmaA_slides` and `V_std_reverse`. They run through the holes `f̃⁻¹(0), f̃⁻¹(1), …`, and the holes are found with
  `List.find?` over an explicit cell list. There is no `Finset.sort` anywhere ([[finset-sort-is-not-kernel-reducible]]).
- `phiWith Rule ranking` is the parametrised form that the lesion uses. `phi := phiWith revRule tilde`.

## Tier C, piece 1 only: Σ in filling form, and thm:G4 as a finite check (tworow-d4-kernel `ce14669`)
- **`sigmaRR`** follows tex l.122–129. It runs `slides fwdRule` on `rr R C (max f) f` over the support `rot(A/B)`. The holes
  are the cells of κ = A^∨ in decreasing `U_κ` order (top row first, right to left), and the labels are `z_k ↦ r_k+1`.
- **`g4_finite_33` (kernel).** On all 310 instances, `checkRow phi` and `checkRow sigmaRR` both hold. So **Φ(f) = Σ(rr f)**
  there, in support and values. Lean Σ∘rr also equals Python Φ on 3556/3556 by `#guard` (evaluation). This is a
  computation, **not** the theorem.
- **Lesion `lesion_sigma_column_order` (kernel).** κ is processed by columns, right column first and top to bottom. This
  order is *legal*: every cell is an inner corner when it is processed. It changes Σ(rr f) at `(2,2,[1],[0])` and on 66/310
  instances (1664/3556 by evaluation).
  - My first attempt at this lesion used the reversed `U_κ` order. That order slides into cells that are not inner corners,
    and its failures (3045/3556) are a broken precondition, not evidence. I discarded it.
- **Planted guards.** I changed the expected count to 1663 and fed the row lesion to Φ in a scratch file. Both error
  (2/2), so the test driver is live.
