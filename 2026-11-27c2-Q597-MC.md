## Theorem (Thm final)
Let M be a Purbhoo mosaic with its Z^4 labels (class 𝓜, 0<m0<n). Let (x,y) be distinct nest-A corners with x→B and y→C, and suppose the four journeys J_x, J_y, J_y^x, J_x^y terminate. Then M·J_x·J_y^x = M·J_y·J_x^y.

## Proof (outline)
1. **Cor mc.** For diverging pairs, (G3′) (c1, Cor g3) gives Δ_x = Δ_y, and Prop mcE with PQ-RIGID gives equal boards. For non-diverging pairs, Δ_x = Δ_y = 0.
2. **Lemma dict** (read off the engine against Purbhoo §2.3 and §3.1). A nest-A corner is an accessible singleton flock. The direction u or w is E or N for that flock, and each step is Purbhoo's hexagon rotation. **The exception is ties**: Purbhoo says the minimal hexagon is unique (l.565), but 29% of census steps have more than one candidate, and our flagged tie rule decides those.
3. **The dictionary is not the lever.** Purbhoo's Thm 1 is about flock labels, and a singleton carries none. **Lemma mosP**: journeys preserve 𝓜. This comes from the engine's definition plus (Hex). (Hex) says every accepted cluster is a convex unit-sided hexagon whose boundary is invariant under the rotation; it is proved by exhaustion over 144 clusters in `hexenum.py`.
4. **Thm S (Surj), the converse of Lemma D.** Every M ∈ 𝓜 is M0(ℓ). Write ψ = π0+π1 and F = ψ∘φ⁻¹. F is orientation-preserving on triangles and squares (Lemma 36, a 36-case table). Nest rhombi collapse onto the sides of T_n, and F(∂H) runs once around ∂T_n. A degree count then shows that the images of the triangles and squares tile T_n. Labels agree across edges, the lift is L by Lemma D(1), and the nests are the bubble cells by the staircase argument.
5. **Lemma F.** 𝓕's labelled-intersection property follows from the edge-to-edge tiling plus injectivity of φ on Z^4.
