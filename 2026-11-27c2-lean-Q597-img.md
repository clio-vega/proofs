- e is interior, because boundary 0-edges map into ∂Δ₀ (`bdry_img`, from D4_walk partial sums);
- `posTri e` is in T_n and is not 000. If it were, N₀ of its image would be ≥ 1 (`nchain_pos`), so by G1 its

- so it has a 10-edge g (`piece_has10`, decide). g is interior because it is not 0/1, so the rhombus is in T_n;
- `rhombus_clause_P` gives a partner 0-edge e′ ≠ e (`pick_other`: countP = 2 on a nodup list) with the same
  class and the same image, and ν(e′) > ν(e). That is 6 concrete cases (class × which edge is 10).

contradicts badness directly.

## Caveat (read before citing clause 3)

M₀ is **not defined in Lean**. Clause 3 is proved for the family L(p_s) + a(D,0) + b(0,D) with a ≤ m₀ and b ≤ m₁.
Definition def:M0 asserts that every nest-rhombus vertex is in this family ("every intermediate word is a
rearrangement of the side's letters"). That assertion belongs to the definition, not to the corollary, and it is
not formalised. Lifted-tile vertices are L(p) with p a vertex of T_n, so clauses 1–2 cover them. The Lean
statement does not need the tex's standing assumption 0 < m₀ < n.
