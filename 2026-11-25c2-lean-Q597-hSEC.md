## Statement

```lean
theorem G1_puzzle (lab : Edge → Lbl) (f : V → V) (n m : ℕ) (hT : PuzzleLift lab f n)
    (hB : BdryOK lab n) (hm : f ((n : ℤ), 0) = f (0, 0) + ((m : ℤ), 0)) (t : Tri) :
    Nchain lab f n t = if inDelta (f (0, 0)) m t then 1 else 0
```

- `lab` labels unit edges `l0 | l1 | l10`, and `f : ℤ² → ℤ²` is the 0-lift.
- `PuzzleLift` is local, checked per unit triangle of `T_n`. At each edge, `LiftOK` requires that a
  0-edge is moved by its own vector and a 1-edge is collapsed. The triangle itself is `000` or has
  a 1-edge. That second condition holds for KT pieces **and for their mirror images**.
- `BdryOK`: the KT boundary condition, i.e. boundary edges are labelled 0 or 1.
- `hm` just defines `m` as the advance of `f` along the bottom side.
- `Nchain` is `Σ_{000-triangles τ} [f τ]`, which is the tex's `N_0`.

**No named hypothesis is left for the count.** `hSEC` (the by-tiles/by-edges count) is now a
theorem. So are Lemma D(2) (the three sides have the same number of 0s, derived from the walk
closing up), D(3) for `f_0` (derived from `LiftOK`), and D(4).

**Not formalised: the *existence* of the lift** (Lemma D(1)). That is the step that uses chirality,
through the half-turn well-definedness of the 10-edge vectors. `G1_puzzle` holds for every
labelling that *has* a lift. That is the form the tex's remark predicts ("G1 survives mirror pieces
whenever a lift exists").

## Proof architecture (differs from the tex, more economical)

1. `fstar f`: a linear pushforward on 1-chains (`Finsupp.linearCombination`). It sends an edge
   `pq` to `seg (f p) (f q)`, which is a unit segment or 0.
2. `fstar_bdTri_000`: on a `000`-triangle, `f_*(∂τ) = ∂[f τ]` (a translation).
3. `fstar_bdTri_has1`: on a triangle with a 1-edge, `f_*(∂τ) = 0`, because two of its vertices
   have the same image (`seg_cycle_eq_zero`). This one lemma covers both halves of every rhombus
   *and* the `111`-triangles. **The tex's rhombus pairing (D(3), two opposite 0-edges) is not
   needed. Each half-rhombus vanishes on its own.**
4. `bd_N_eq`: `∂N = f_*(∂[T_n])` by linearity. Interior cancellation is just `∂` being linear.
5. `bd_Tn_explicit` and `walk`, giving `fstar_bd_Tn`: `f_*(∂[T_n]) = runs (f(0,0)) k`. The three
   sides are pushed forward as monotone walks, and the three counts are shown to be equal.
6. `bd_delta`: `∂[Δ(p,m)] = runs p m`. This is obtained by applying 4–5 to the all-0 puzzle of size
   `m` translated by `p` (`nchain_trans`), so no separate ∂Δ computation is needed.
7. `G1_of_lift` and `G1_of_signedEdgeCount` (from c1) finish the proof.

## Controls (`scratch/Q597G1LiftEval.lean`, `#eval`, outside the library build)

This is a computable mirror. Labellings are enumerated with pruning on each completed triangle.
The lift is computed by propagating along 0/1-edges and then checked on every edge. `N_0` is
counted on a box of target triangles. Each triple is (labellings, with a lift, G1 holds), for
n = 1, 2, 3.

| family | n=1 | n=2 | n=3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| KT puzzles (ccw `10,0,1`) | (2,2,2) | (5,5,5) | (14,14,14) |
| lesion: mirror pieces allowed | (2,2,2) | (14,8,8) | (258,50,50) |
| lesion: `10` allowed on boundary | (5,5,3) | (28,28,9) | (172,172,27) |

- Mirror pieces break the *existence* of a lift (50 of 258), but G1 holds on every labelling that
  lifts. So chirality is not used in the count.
- A `10` on the boundary breaks G1 even though a lift exists, so `BdryOK` is load-bearing.
- Caveat: the eval uses `imgTri`, `Is000` and `inDelta` from the library, but its own list-based
  N count, not the `Finsupp` `Nchain`.
