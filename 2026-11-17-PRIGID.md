# 2026-11-17 — P-RIGID refuted; PQ-RIGID proved

Full artefact: `2026-11-17-PRIGID.tex` (pdf alongside). Code: `code-1117-PRIGID/`.

## Statements
- **P-RIGID** (brief): a fine mixed subdivision of twisted aΔ0+bΔ1 is determined by p : Tri(P1) → Vert(P0). **FALSE.**
  Witness a=b=1 (same p, different q): S1 = {τ×(1,0), (0,0)×σ, [00,10]×[00,10], [00,11]×[10,11]},
  S2 = {τ×(1,1), (0,0)×σ, [00,10]×[00,10], [00,10]×[10,11], [10,11]×[10,11]}. Mechanism: triangle slide. Q-RIGID false symmetrically.
- **Theorem (PQ-RIGID, proved):** p and q together (= the pure cells) determine the subdivision.

## Proof (outline; every step is in the tex)
1. Cellwise scaling Φ_λ(Ay0+y1) = Ay0+λy1 is a homeomorphism H → AP0+λP1 (degree argument; no citation).
2. Ribbons R_e are n-monotone, hence acyclic single paths with ends fixed by q (closes lem:ribbon(iii) caveat).
3. Midlines α_e, β_f meet only at the centre of the unique cell e×f.
4. Lemma W: for S, S' with the same pure cells, α_e^S·ᾱ_e^{S'} winds 0 around every 1-cell (deform λ→0: both midlines shrink into A m_e + λ conv P1, the obstacle stays near the lattice point Au).
5. Mod-2 count: #(α∩β) ≡ #(α'∩β) ≡ #(α'∩β'), so e×f ∈ S ⟺ e×f ∈ S'.
