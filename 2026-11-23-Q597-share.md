# 2026-11-23 PROVE — Q597 (Share): the two contact hexagons have the same square

Source: `2026-11-23-Q597-share.tex` (pdf beside it). Code: `code-1123-Q597/`.

**Theorem (proved).** Let J_x, J_y be terminating journeys on a Purbhoo mosaic M0, toward any targets (B/C, B/B, C/C), that read each other's wakes. Then the contact hexagons H^x_{p_x} and H^y_{p_y} have the same square, a tile of M0. Consequences: (Share), and c0(x)=p_y, c0(y)=p_x (via Lemma hs, 11-22 c2).

**Proof idea: a monotone cycle.**
- Common potential (proved by hand from MU and the Δλ table): Π_B = (λ0, λ1, −ψ_B1, −ψ_B0+λ1) and Π_C = (λ0, λ1, λ0−ψ_C1, −ψ_C0).
  - Both translate by the same vector (D0, D2, −D1, D2+D3).
  - Each is non-decreasing along every journey toward its target.
  - A step is stationary iff it is a class-0 b-step.
- Square sandwich (finite check, 12 rows): if J removes square u at step k and J' removes it at step j, then Π(R'_j) ≤ Π(R_{k+1}). Equality only for the σ2 = [(1,0)|(1,−1)] square with J' at (0,b).
- If the squares u ≠ v, each is removed by the other journey strictly after its contact. That gives the chain Π(R^y_{p_y+1}) ≤ Π(R^y_j) ≤ Π(R^x_{p_x+1}) ≤ Π(R^x_{j'}) ≤ Π(R^y_{p_y+1}), so every step is an equality. Equality forces step p_x+1 of J_x to be a (0,b), from class 0. But a σ2-removing step lands on class 1. Contradiction.

**Lemma sq (proved by hand).** The square of the contact hexagon lies in the wake. The re-run takes a different good configuration at R_p. The square of G_t overlaps a tile of G_t' (finite, 24 rows), and that tile is on the re-run's tiling Y_p. So the square is off Y_p, hence in X_p \ Y_p = W (Lemma dinv). Theorem local is no longer used; it survives as an independent cross-check.
